ABOUT HESPERUS GAMES

We are a tiny game development studio based in Aotearoa New Zealand.

We are currently developing Death at Sycamore Hall, a detective and language deduction game set in a wizard academy.

A Case of Fraud, a document-based narrative deduction game, is available now on Steam.

A Case of Fraud is a non-linear, document-based investigation and deduction game.

Every clue is hidden in plain sight. Carefully read and cross-reference documents to unravel a CEO’s disappearance and expose a case of fraud.

DEATH AT SYCAMORE HALL

INVESTIGATION / DETECTIVE / MYSTERY


Available to wishlist on Steam


Wishlist on Steam Press Kit

Death at Sycamore Hall is a narrative deduction game with language learning and magic.

Explore an ill-fated magical academy. Find traces of magic and deduce the meaning of incantations. Examine scenes for clues to help you piece together the story, and determine the identity and fate of each former resident.
